## Wren landing page

The Klarity landing page is adapted from [Wren](https://github.com/ln-dev7/landing-pages/tree/master/landing-pages/wren), designed and developed by LN (https://lndev.me/), from the ln-dev7/landing-pages repository.

Adapted portions include the landing-page layout and CSS, topographic contour SVG generator, typed headline, scroll reveal, section tracking, workspace window, interactive section layouts, and product cards. Klarity replaces the concept-product content and interactions, integrates its own routing and brand components, scopes the styles, maps colors to the dashboard theme, and uses its shared GSAP sliding-pill animation.
